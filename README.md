## ✨ What is AppFresh

AppFresh is a native Mac app manager. It scans your installed apps, checks updates from the App Store, Homebrew, and developer websites in one place, and can uninstall apps together with their associated files. The UI is available in Simplified Chinese and English.

## 🚀 Features

### 🔎 Check Updates in One Place
Scan installed apps and check the App Store, Homebrew, and developer websites together — see every available update and what changed in one place.

### ⚡ Update with a Click
Update the right way per source: the App Store via the built-in mas, Homebrew via brew, and official packages after verification. AppFresh itself updates automatically through Sparkle.

### 🧹 Uninstall & Clean Up
Remove apps you no longer need along with their associated files — caches, preferences, containers, and more. Sensitive data like browser profiles and chat history stay protected until you confirm.

### 🧭 Manage Installed Apps
See all your installed apps in one place. Filter by source, sort by name, size, recent use, or install date, and check version, size, and where each app came from.

## 🤔 Why AppFresh

macOS app updates are scattered across the App Store, Homebrew, and developer websites — checking each is tedious and easy to miss, and unwanted apps leave behind caches and preferences. AppFresh brings it all together and adds uninstall with cleanup, so your favorites stay fresh and the rest leaves cleanly.
